BadUSB - On Accessories that Turn Evil by Karsten Nohl + Jakob Lell

HackerTube summary: Karsten Nohl and Jakob Lell present BadUSB at Black Hat 2014, showing that USB controller firmware can be reprogrammed to impersonate other device types.
